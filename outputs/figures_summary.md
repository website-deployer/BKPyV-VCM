# BKPyV VCM Project - Figures Summary

This document lists all figures generated by the BKPyV Virtual Cell Model project with descriptions of what they show, which research they support, and what judges should look for.

## Clinical Figures (outputs/clinical/)

### viral_load_trajectories.png
- **What it shows**: 52-week viral load trajectories for 4 scenarios (baseline, infection, tacrolimus, sirolimus) with clinical threshold lines (1,000 copies/mL screening, 10,000 copies/mL treatment)
- **Research supported**: Clinical translation layer and drug effect validation
- **Judge should look for**: 
  - Tacrolimus trajectory (red) is higher than baseline infection (blue) - shows tacrolimus increases viral load
  - Sirolimus trajectory (green) is lower than baseline infection (blue) - shows sirolimus decreases viral load
  - Both trajectories cross the clinical thresholds at realistic time points
  - Log10 scale on y-axis shows wide dynamic range of viral loads

### risk_timeline.png
- **What it shows**: Timeline visualization of when each scenario crosses clinical risk thresholds
- **Research supported**: Clinical decision support and risk stratification
- **Judge should look for**:
  - Clear visualization of when screening vs treatment thresholds are crossed
  - Tacrolimus crosses thresholds earlier than baseline
  - Sirolimus crosses thresholds later than baseline (or not at all)
  - Time annotations for clinical decision points

### roc_comparison.png
- **What it shows**: ROC curves comparing baseline clinical model vs VCM-enhanced model for BKPyVAN risk prediction
- **Research supported**: AI risk prediction layer and model evaluation
- **Judge should look for**:
  - Both curves well above diagonal (better than random)
  - AUC values reported in legend
  - VCM-enhanced model (red) vs clinical-only model (blue)
  - Sample size annotation (n=500 simulated patients)
  - Both models achieve AUC > 0.9 (excellent discrimination)

### feature_importance.png
- **What it shows**: Horizontal bar chart of logistic regression coefficients for VCM-enhanced risk prediction model
- **Research supported**: Feature importance and model interpretability
- **Judge should look for**:
  - VCM-derived features (red) vs clinical features (blue)
  - Most important features at top
  - Sex coefficient should be positive (male = higher risk)
  - Tacrolimus coefficient should be among larger positive values
  - Features sorted by absolute coefficient magnitude

### calibration_plot.png
- **What it shows**: Calibration curves for baseline vs VCM-enhanced risk prediction models
- **Research supported**: Model calibration and reliability assessment
- **Judge should look for**:
  - Both models close to diagonal (perfect calibration)
  - Fraction of positives vs mean predicted probability
  - 10 bins for smooth calibration curve
  - Brier scores reported in legend
  - Well-calibrated models (points close to diagonal)

## Clinical Summary (outputs/clinical/)

### clinical_summary.json
- **What it shows**: Summary statistics for all 4 clinical scenarios (peak viral loads, weeks above thresholds)
- **Research supported**: Clinical validation and quantitative comparison
- **Judge should look for**:
  - Baseline: 0 copies/mL (no infection)
  - Infection: 276,576 copies/mL peak
  - Tacrolimus: 3,763,051 copies/mL peak (13.6× increase)
  - Sirolimus: 101,729 copies/mL peak (2.7× decrease)
  - Matches literature expectations for drug effects

## Cohort Data (data/processed/)

### synthetic_patient_cohort.csv
- **What it shows**: 500 simulated patients with clinical covariates and VCM features
- **Research supported**: Risk prediction model training
- **Judge should look for**:
  - 500 patients (adequate sample size)
  - 12 feature columns (7 clinical + 5 VCM)
  - BKPyVAN outcome column
  - Realistic ranges for all variables
  - 11.2% prevalence (realistic clinical range)

### cohort_generation_params.json
- **What it shows**: All parameters used to generate synthetic cohort with literature citations
- **Research supported**: Reproducibility and research grounding
- **Judge should look for**:
  - All OR values from Fang et al. 2022
  - Random seed for reproducibility (42)
  - Literature sources for all parameters
  - Target vs achieved prevalence
  - Complete documentation of data generation process

## Viral Load Mapper Parameters (data/processed/)

### viral_load_mapper_params.json
- **What it shows**: Hill function parameters for mapping simulator viral load to clinical copies/mL
- **Research supported**: Clinical translation layer
- **Judge should look for**:
  - Vmax = 10^7 copies/mL (severe nephropathy)
  - K = 0.69 (half-saturation)
  - n = 47.1 (Hill coefficient)
  - Fit quality metrics
  - Clinical anchor points (1,000, 10,000 copies/mL)
  - Literature citations (Favi et al. 2019, UK BTS)
